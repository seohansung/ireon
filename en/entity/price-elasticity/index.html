price elasticity

Cross-price elasticity of demand measures the percentage change in quantity demanded of a good relative to the percentage change in the price of a related good.

mechanism

Mechanism

Cross-price elasticity of demand measures the percentage change in quantity demanded of a good relative to the percentage change in the price of a related good. This calculation involves dividing the percentage change in quantity demanded by the percentage change in price. The resulting value indicates how responsive the demand for one good is to price changes in another good. price elasticity is derived from this process by applying the same percentage change methodology to a single good's price.
