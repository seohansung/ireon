marginal utility

definition

Definition

marginal utility refers to the additional utility gained from consuming one more unit of a good. The marginal utility per dollar is calculated by dividing the additional utility by the product's price. At the point of optimal choice, this ratio is equal for both goods considered. This equality ensures that the consumer allocates spending to maximize satisfaction. The concept applies when comparing different goods and their respective utility values.
