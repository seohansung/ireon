chain rule

definition

Definition

chain rule is a method for finding the derivative of composite functions. It involves differentiating composites of three or more functions by applying the chain rule multiple times. The rule allows combining it with other differentiation techniques to find derivatives of complex functions. The process requires identifying the outer and inner functions within the composite structure. This method is essential for handling more intricate functions that involve multiple layers of differentiation.

Mechanism

chain rule can be expressed using Leibniz's notation, which is consistent with how other derivatives are represented.
