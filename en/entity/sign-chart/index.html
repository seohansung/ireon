sign chart

Using derivatives

A sign chart is a graphical representation used to illustrate the behavior of a function's derivatives, specifically indicating where a function is increasing or decreasing through the analysis of its first derivative, and where it is concave up or concave down through the analysis of its second derivative. This tool provides a structured method for summarizing critical information about the shape and direction of change in a function.
