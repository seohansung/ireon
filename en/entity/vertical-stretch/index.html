Definition

vertical stretch refers to a transformation where the graph of a function is scaled vertically by a specified factor. This transformation stretches the graph away from the x-axis, increasing the distance of each point from the axis. It is distinct from horizontal reflections and other transformations like horizontal stretches or reflections. The factor determines the extent of the vertical scaling, with larger factors resulting in more pronounced stretching.
