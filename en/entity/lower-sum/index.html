definition

Definition

lower sum refers to a specific type of Riemann sum where function values are evaluated at the left endpoints of subintervals. It is one of the special kinds of approximations used in integral calculations. Left-endpoint approximations contribute to forming lower sums by selecting the minimum function value within each subinterval.
