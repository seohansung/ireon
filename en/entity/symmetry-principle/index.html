Definition

symmetry principle states that a region symmetric with respect to a line has its centroid lying on that line. It also relates to volume calculation through the theorem of Pappus, which connects the region's area to the volume generated by revolving it around an external axis. The principle aids in determining centroids for symmetric regions by leveraging their inherent symmetry.
