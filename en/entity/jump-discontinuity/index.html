definition

Definition

jump discontinuity is a noninfinite discontinuity where the function's sections do not meet up. It is characterized by the existence of left and right limits that are not equal. This type of discontinuity occurs when the graph has a sudden break, and the function is left continuous but not continuous at certain points. The domain of the function is specified as [0, 5].
