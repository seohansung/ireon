cylindrical shell

[cylindrical shell|cylindrical shell] is a method used to calculate the volume of a solid of revolution.

definition

Definition

cylindrical shell is a method used to calculate the volume of a solid of revolution. This technique involves integrating cylindrical shells around the axis of rotation. The method is particularly effective for solids formed by rotating a function around an axis. It provides an alternative approach to the disk and washer methods.
