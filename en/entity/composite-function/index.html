Definition

composite function The composite function theorem establishes a relationship between functions f and g when the range of f is a subset of the domain of g. This theorem is invaluable in demonstrating the continuity of trigonometric functions. A composite function (g ∘ f)(x) is defined as g(f(x)) where f has domain A and range B, and g has domain D and range E with B ⊆ D.
