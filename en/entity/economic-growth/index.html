Definition

economic growth refers to rapid and sustained increases in a country's economic output. This phenomenon is relatively recent for the human race, particularly in high-income nations where continuous growth requires advancing education to foster innovation and technological application. Economic growth in these countries faces the challenge of maintaining progress through workforce development and investment in new technologies.
