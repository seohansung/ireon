Privacy Policy

Ireon is designed to be readable without accounts. This page explains how basic analytics may be used.

No account required

You can read Ireon without creating an account. Ireon does not directly ask visitors to submit personal information.

Analytics

Ireon may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand traffic patterns and improve page quality.

Cookies

Analytics tools may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, page views, and general usage behavior.

Service improvement

Collected analytics are used to improve the site, understand which pages are useful, and guide future content development.

What may be measured

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate geographic region
  • Traffic source
  • Time spent on pages
  • General device and browser information

Data use

Ireon does not sell personal information. Analytics data is used to improve the service, monitor site performance, and understand how readers discover and use the site.
