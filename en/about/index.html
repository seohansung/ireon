About Ireon

Ireon is an entity-centered knowledge engine that turns structured source material into connected concept pages.

Entity-centered

Ireon treats each concept as an entity. A concept is not only named; it is explained, connected, and placed inside a larger structure of knowledge.

Connected knowledge

Ideas are linked to related ideas. This helps readers move from one concept to another and understand how knowledge forms a network.

Structured explanation

Pages are organized around definition, mechanism, effects, comparison, examples, and related concepts instead of isolated dictionary entries.

Generated from evidence

Ireon is built from structured source evidence. The system analyzes educational material and recomposes it into readable concept pages.

Why Ireon exists

Knowledge is often scattered across chapters, paragraphs, and examples. Ireon reorganizes that material into connected concept pages so that readers can explore ideas more naturally.

The goal is simple: make complex knowledge easier to search, compare, and understand.
